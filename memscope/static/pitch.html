The substrate · The moat

Connectors are commodity.
The substrate is the moat.

Four properties of the layer underneath — each takes years to replicate, and together they're what every AI tool will eventually need.
01

Pay once, never twice

Every chunk is keyed by a hash of its content. Re-ingesting the same paragraph costs zero — embeddings, storage, indexing, all skipped.

Why moatAt scale, this is the whole infra bill.
02

Permissions that can't leak

The permission filter runs in SQL, before search ranks anything. Forbidden content is never ranked, never counted, never returned.

Why moatEvery memory product eventually faces a CISO. This is the answer.
03

Conflict resolution built in

Memory is append-only with source precedence: human > system > agent. A low-confidence agent guess literally cannot overwrite a human correction.

Why moatTrust degrades fast without this; trust enables everything.
04

Hybrid retrieval

Vector + full-text + memory rows, fused with Reciprocal Rank Fusion. ACL applied inside both arms — not bolted on after.

Why moatSolves the "retrieved a thing but it's stale, wrong, or forbidden" failure mode.
